Subdivision of share capital

Author
Lossiemouth Limited
Date
16 October 2026

On 16 October 2026, Keith McKenzie, the sole member, passed an ordinary resolution to subdivide the Company’s one issued ordinary share of £1.00 into 100 ordinary shares of £0.01 each. The aggregate nominal value remains £1.00, and Keith McKenzie’s proportionate holding is unchanged.

The related SH02 notice sets out the subdivision and resulting share capital.
